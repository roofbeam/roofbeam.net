The thesis

why any of this

The name comes from Sappho by way of Salinger — Raise High the Roof Beam, Carpenters. When the giant is coming, the carpenters raise the roof to make room for him.

That is the wager: the world should make space for its giants rather than cut them down to fit the room — outward, in what gets built and who gets to build it, and inward, in the room a person keeps for themselves.

The work here follows from that. Tools that give people back the power to act on what they already know. Ideas put out in the open so they can be argued with. Nothing here is built to be admired.

The ground it is worked out on, mostly Stoic:

  • Discourses & Enchiridion — Epictetus
  • Letters on Ethics — Seneca
  • Meditations — Marcus Aurelius
  • The Inner Citadel — Pierre Hadot

How it's governed

who decides what

Roofbeam LLC is an Oregon company, formed in 2026. It holds the costs and the agreements so the projects underneath it do not each have to.

The work happens in the open. Membership is a separate and deliberate step — looking is easy, joining is a choice, and it is meant to mean something.
