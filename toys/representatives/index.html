Congress: unitedstates/congress-legislators (public domain). State legislators and executives: Open States (CC0). President, Cabinet, Supreme Court and state high courts: Wikidata (CC0). Districts: U.S. Census geocoder.

The Cabinet here is the heads of the fifteen executive departments; which other posts count as "Cabinet-level" changes with each administration.

Some cards say less than others. State legislators have no tenure or committees in Open States' bulk files; some states' elected executives aren't in Open States yet; and no open source keeps state supreme court justices current, so each state's high court gets one card that points to who sits there now. Those gaps are the data's, not a judgement.
