Tribes of Behavior

Not the map of who governed but of what people did — the tongues, creeds, work and media that grew across the globe like molds on a peach. Each region's history is a separate account; where accounts overlap, the place's own wins and the other is kept beside it. Early on every network must touch; watch contiguity break once signals outrun bodies. Scroll or pinch to zoom, click to pin a point and read its fiber.

      Layers

      How it's drawn. Each account is one region's telling. A tribe is a handful of colonies in it — a seed point, a reach and a strength, keyed to years — that add up. Where two accounts describe the same tribe, the smallest region containing the point decides, even when it says "nothing here"; outside every telling region, the strongest account wins. Nothing is merged in the data: the panel on the right shows both.

      Every value names its source. Today they are all sketches, marked speculative, waiting for real citations. The rules themselves are versioned functions (release.json); the geos and their coverage are at geos.html.
