Tribes of Behavior

Not the map of who governed Iberia but of what people there did — the tongues, creeds and work that grew across the peninsula like molds on a peach. Early on every network must touch, because ideas travel inside bodies. Watch what happens to contiguity once signals outrun bodies. Click anywhere to pin a point and read its fiber: the bundle of behaviors an observer could see there, at that moment.

    Layers

    How it's drawn. Each tribe is a handful of colonies: a seed point, a reach and a strength, keyed to years and interpolated between. Overlapping colonies merge; where two tribes share ground, both show. That is the point — a place is rarely one thing.

    Shapes are approximations from general histories. Treat edges as soft and dates as ± a generation. The data is one editable file, bundles.json.
