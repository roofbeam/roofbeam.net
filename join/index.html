On your phone? Get the app

After you've joined, the app lets you get messages like a text. You only do this once.

  1. Install Zulip. It's free.
    iPhone Android
  2. Open it and tap Add account (or Enter your Zulip server URL).
  3. Type this address exactly:
    chat.roofbeam.net
  4. Tap Log in with email, and use the email and password you just chose.
  5. When it asks about notifications, tap Allow.

Stuck at any step? That's on us, not you. Reply to whoever sent you the link and they'll set it up with you.
